About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MadxCrazyTech handles information when you use Arrow Puzzle.

Arrow Puzzle does not require account creation or registration. Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The Game also uses Google AdMob, which may process certain information to provide advertisements.

1. Information Collection

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Gameplay information, including level progress, coins, booster inventory, and sound and vibration preferences, is stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to our own servers.

Local gameplay data may be removed if you clear the app's storage, reset your device, or uninstall the Game.

B. Information Processed by Third-Party Services

We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number through account registration. However, third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically process certain information.

Google AdMob: Arrow Puzzle uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Depending on the service configuration, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • Device information and advertising identifiers.
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address, where applicable.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and other advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, where collected by integrated services.

The information processed depends on the SDK versions, enabled features, consent configuration, and applicable Google policies.

2. Permissions

Arrow Puzzle may use device permissions needed for its features and functionality. Permissions requested by the Game are intended to support those features. Review the permission prompts on your device to understand what is requested.

3. Data Security

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers to store your gameplay information.

We aim to use appropriate practices to protect information. Third-party services manage their own data security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Data Backup and Recovery

Arrow Puzzle does not provide developer-operated cloud backup or account synchronization. If you clear the app's storage, reset your device, or uninstall the Game, your saved progress may be permanently lost.

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob provides advertising features in Arrow Puzzle. Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps or sites that use its services
  • Google Advertising Technologies

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your Android device settings or Google Ads Settings . Available controls vary by device, account, and region. Changing ad preferences may affect personalization but does not necessarily remove ads.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle is a casual puzzle game intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may process information as described in this policy. We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play policies.

If you have concerns about a child's privacy, please contact us through the official app listing or available support channels.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official app listing or available support channels.
